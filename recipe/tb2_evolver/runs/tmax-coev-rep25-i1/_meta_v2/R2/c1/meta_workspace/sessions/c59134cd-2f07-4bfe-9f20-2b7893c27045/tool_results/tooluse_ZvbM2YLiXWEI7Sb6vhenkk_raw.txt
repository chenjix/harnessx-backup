44	    `[LoopDetection] ⚠️ The exact same tool call(s) have been issued 10

51	- Why Control not Instruction: the failure is a *mechanical* runaway —
52	  the model re-emits a byte-identical call and cannot see it is looping;
53	  a prompt rule ("don't repeat yourself") does not fire reliably inside
54	  the loop. A hook that fingerprints tool calls and injects a warning /

56	  observable repeat state. Not Action: no new capability is needed; Bash
57	  already suffices.
58	- Why disable Strategy-2 (name-only warn): this benchmark is Bash-only,
59	  so name-only fingerprints match on *every* consecutive Bash call
60	  regardless of arguments. Strategy 2 therefore fires on normal
61	  long-horizon exploration, not loops. Verified: c1's two real losses
62	  (1536, 1652) both show `[LoopDetection] ⚠️ You have called Bash N times
63	  consecutively with different arguments` (name-only warn) firing on
64	  legitimate multi-step work, after which the agent prematurely declared
65	  "task complete" / "let me proceed efficiently" and abandoned remaining
66	  requirements — R0 solved these in 76/88 msgs, c1 cut them to 38/64 and
67	  failed. Setting `name_warn_threshold` above any realistic step count
68	  keeps Strategy 1 (exact-repeat, high-precision) and removes Strategy 2.
69	- Retroactive check (C-preservative-transfer): yes — 338 shows the
70	  exact-repeat warn firing at the decisive step and unblocking a correct
71	  commit; 015's byte-identical loop would have been raised at threshold
72	  12. Both ends grounded (habit fires on 338; absent-and-needed on 015).
73	- expected_global_gain: recovers the degenerate exact-repeat loop

94	- Tasks affected:
95	  - passing under c4 (gate fired and held): task_000740_59416444,
96	    task_001264_9f4ca84a, task_001498_df8254c9, task_001673_86224c91,

101	  exited unverified → reward 0.
102	- Verified (Read): c4 965 trajectory shows the gate's checklist

104	  injected as a tool result; c4's 5 gains all pass after the bounded
105	  re-nudge. R0 gate is `CustomSelfVerifyProcessor` (one-shot) — the five
106	  gains were R0 failures.
107	- Why Control not Instruction: the gate must *intercept the exit event*

112	- Retroactive check (B-preservative-lock): yes — removing the gate (i.e.

114	  five cited tasks (they are R0 zeros).
115	- expected_global_gain: locks in c4's +5 verified-completion cluster.
116	- regression_risk: the gate can over-nudge an already-correct solution
117	  into churn — this is exactly c4's one real loss (965: agent had correct
118	  variance, gate re-nudged, agent over-edited Welford and broke it).
119	  `max_nudges=2` bounds it; C-001's exact-repeat guard also catches any
120	  re-edit loop the extra nudge induces. Accepted as a known, bounded cost
121	  against +5 gains.
122	- cost_shift: small INCREASE on tasks that get re-nudged (bounded by
123	  max_nudges=2); net positive given the 5:1 gain:loss ratio.
