22	### Why
23	
24	Baseline 20/50 (0.40). Beyond the large `budget_exceeded` thrash cluster
25	(~12 tasks stuck in repetition loops on genuinely hard crypto / reverse-
26	engineering / multi-service tasks — model capability gaps, not harness),
27	there is a clean **harness-shaped** cluster: 4 tasks whose verifier aborts
28	at **pytest collection time** with `ModuleNotFoundError` — the grader does
29	`import requests` / `import imageio` against a container whose base image
30	lacks the package. Solution quality is irrelevant; the test file cannot
31	even be collected, so `reward=0`.
32	
33	The R0 config already carried a `VerifierDepGuardProcessor`, but it fired
34	**only on the agent's clean exit-intent turn** and its allow-list was just
35	`(requests, pyyaml)`. Trajectory grep of `VERIFIER DEP CHECK` shows it
36	fired on the 36 clean-exit tasks but on **none** of the 3 ImportError
37	tasks that ended via `budget_exceeded` / `error` — the exact tasks that
38	needed it. And task_000773 (a clean finish) still failed because `imageio`
39	was not in the allow-list.
40	
41	### Changes
42	
43	- `processors/verifier_dep_guard.py` — rewrote `VerifierDepGuardProcessor`
44	  to fire **once early** (first `on_after_model`), appending a forced Bash

46	  gating on a graceful exit. Expanded allow-list to the ubiquitous verifier
47	  libs: `requests, imageio, Pillow(PIL), numpy, scipy, pyyaml`. Still a
