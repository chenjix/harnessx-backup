29	## Global optimization constraint (Pareto-style)
30	
31	Do not optimize a narrow local win at the expense of global benchmark health. Prefer candidates that improve failing clusters while protecting already-passing clusters.
32	
33	For each shipped candidate, explicitly state:
34	- `expected_global_gain`: which failing cluster(s) and why this can generalize
35	- `regression_risk`: what could break outside `predicted_affected`
36	- `cost_shift`: expected token/cost movement if the change lands

38	A local improvement with likely net global degradation is not acceptable unless you provide unusually strong evidence and a clear rollback trigger.
