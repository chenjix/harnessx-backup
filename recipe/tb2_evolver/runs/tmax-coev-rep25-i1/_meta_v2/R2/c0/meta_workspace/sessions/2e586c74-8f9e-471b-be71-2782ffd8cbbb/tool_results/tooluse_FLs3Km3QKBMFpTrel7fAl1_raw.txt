20	### Why

22	R0 (reward 0.500) failure clusters are dominated by heredoc-based
23	whole-file rewrites and duplicate-consecutive-command repetition
24	loops. FAIL clusters showed 190 dup-commands / 28 EditDetection
25	warnings vs 96 / 1 on PASS. The anchor `task_000118_3043e92d`

30	EditDetection guard on body tokens. The Tmax evolve path honors

32	the agent real file tools.
