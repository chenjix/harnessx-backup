18	### Why
19	
20	R0 scored 30/50. Five tasks all hit `exit_reason=budget_exceeded` at the
21	full 80-step cap (task_000010, task_000118, task_000264, task_001031,
22	task_001321). The shared root cause is a *reasoning-only truncation spiral*:
23	the model generates verbose narration that exceeds the per-turn `max_tokens`
24	(4096) cap with NO tool call, the run loop appends a passive "cut off by the
25	token limit. Please continue from where you left off." nudge, and the same
26	runaway generation re-primes turn after turn. task_000118 shows the assistant
27	emitting the IDENTICAL sentence ~11 times with zero tool calls; 20 of its
28	user turns are the passive continue nudge. The R0 config carried a text-only
29	`LengthTruncationRecoveryProcessor` that (a) triggered only on
30	`finish_reason=="length"` (which the backend did not report reliably — the
31	raw passive nudge survived into history, proving the processor never fired)
32	and (b) escalated with TEXT the model narrates straight past. task_000010
33	additionally exposed the "correct-logic wrong-path" hard-fail: it wrote
34	`/home/user/k8s_operator.py` while the verifier required
35	`/home/user/operator.py`.
36	
37	### Changes
38	
39	- `processors/length_recovery_forceact.py` — new `MultiHookProcessor`
40	  (singleton group `tmax_length_recovery`, so it REPLACES the R0 processor):
41	  adds a secondary content-length trigger and, after N consecutive
42	  truncations, mechanically injects a real generic Bash workspace-snapshot
43	  tool call so model-external ground truth lands in context and breaks the
44	  spiral. (C-001)
45	- `config.yaml` — swap the R0
46	  `recipe.tmax_eval.processors.length_recovery.LengthTruncationRecoveryProcessor`
47	  entry for the new `file://` processor with
48	  `repeat_threshold=2, force_action_threshold=3, content_char_threshold=40000`.
49	- `system_prompt.txt` — two general strategy rules: verify every required
50	  output artifact exists at its EXACT path before finishing; after 2+
51	  identical failures, stop retrying variants and switch approach; keep turns
52	  short. (C-002)

68	### Uncertainty
69	
70	The forced-snapshot mechanism is proven to fire in this harness
71	(`tb2_self_verify` uses the same injected-tool-call path), but whether fresh
72	state actually lets these particular Qwen3.5-9B runs finish within remaining
73	budget is the open question — some tasks (recursive-CTE perf, MPI) may also
74	have a genuine capability gap the harness cannot close. If the cluster does
75	not shrink at all in R2, the spiral is not the only blocker and the next round
76	should look upstream (per-turn max_tokens sizing, or task-class capability).
