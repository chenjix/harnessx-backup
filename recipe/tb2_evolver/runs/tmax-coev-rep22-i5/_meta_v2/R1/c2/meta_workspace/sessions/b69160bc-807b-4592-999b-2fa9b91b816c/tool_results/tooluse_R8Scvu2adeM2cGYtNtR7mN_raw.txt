### Uncertainty

The escalating nudge may not be enough for a model that ignores in-context
corrections (the CyclicLoopBreaker warnings were already ignored here); the
`break_threshold` hard-stop guarantees the run ends cleanly rather than
burning to `error`, but on its own does not write the output files. The bet is
that firing a strong redirect at repeat #3 (much earlier than the current
pipeline's intermittent warnings) returns enough live budget for the agent to
abandon the tangent. If the target still fails as `loop_detected` with no
output, the next round should look upstream at why the agent could not parse
the WAV header (possible capability gap, not a harness fix).
