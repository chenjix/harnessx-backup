22	### Why
23	
24	Baseline 20/50 (0.40). Beyond the large `budget_exceeded` thrash cluster

26	engineering / multi-service tasks — model capability gaps, not harness),

31	even be collected, so `reward=0`.
32	
33	The R0 config already carried a `VerifierDepGuardProcessor`, but it fired
34	**only on the agent's clean exit-intent turn** and its allow-list was just
35	`(requests, pyyaml)`. Trajectory grep of `VERIFIER DEP CHECK` shows it
36	fired on the 36 clean-exit tasks but on **none** of the 3 ImportError
37	tasks that ended via `budget_exceeded` / `error` — the exact tasks that

39	was not in the allow-list.

104	### Why
105	
106	R0=20/50, R1=20/50 (flat; R1's +4/-4 task swap classified as run-to-run
107	noise). The dominant *harness-shaped* signal in R1 is a content-repetition

119	### Changes
120	
121	- `processors/content_repetition_recovery.py::ContentRepetitionRecoveryProcessor`

125	  (change tactic / gather new evidence), escalating at `escalate_threshold=5`
126	  to a hard "STOP — write the required output file to its exact path or run one

128	  avoids tripping on terse action turns. Counter resets after each nudge.

130	  carry R1's VerifierDepGuardProcessor forward byte-for-byte (repointed to

178	### Why
179	
180	R0=20/50, R1=20/50, R2=21/50. The dominant harness-shaped signal is the
181	budget_exceeded loop cluster (~9 tasks, all pinned at the 80-step cap). R2
