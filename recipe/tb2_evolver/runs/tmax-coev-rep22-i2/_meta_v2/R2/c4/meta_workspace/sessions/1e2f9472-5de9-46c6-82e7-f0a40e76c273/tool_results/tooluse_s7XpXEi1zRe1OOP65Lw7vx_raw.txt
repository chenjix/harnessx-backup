20	### Why
21	
22	Assigned focus task_000011_d089ef35 (scientific_computing) failed after 79
23	steps / 962s in a strict A-B-A-B tool-call loop: the model kept "verifying" its
24	MSE server by re-running two Bash commands that returned byte-identical output
25	each time (`Q0: 8.25` and `Q0..Q3`), interleaved with token-limit cut-offs, for
26	~10 full cycles, and never terminated on its own. The existing
27	`LengthTruncationRecoveryProcessor` never engaged because these turns *carry
28	tool calls* (its consecutive counter resets on any tool call). The
29	`LoopDetectionProcessor` is not even in the pipeline, and its exact detector
30	only counts period-1 consecutive repeats — the alternating A-B pattern resets
31	its count to 1 every step. task_000010_644ab1c2 shows the same class:
32	fingerprint repeated 17× → budget_exceeded at 80 steps / 787s. This is a
33	genuine harness deficiency: a degenerate multi-step cycle that no current
34	mechanism terminates.
35	
36	The underlying wrong MSE values in task_000011 (Q2/Q3 computed as 38.25/55.25
37	instead of 68.25/93.25) are a model reasoning/capability gap, not a harness gap
38	— no prompt injection should hand-feed the correct extraction. Skipped as
39	capability gap; the harness fix targets only the non-termination.
40	
41	### Changes
42	
43	- `processors/cyclic_loop.py` — new `CyclicToolLoopDetector` MultiHookProcessor.
44	  Fingerprints (tool_name, tool_input, result); detects a repeating cycle of
45	  period 1..max_period at the window tail; warns at 2 identical cycles, raises
46	  `LoopDetectedError` (clean exit_reason=loop_detected) at 3.
47	- `config.yaml` — register the processor after PostCompactionRefreshProcessor.
