58	# Degenerate repeated-tool-call loop guard. R1 c1: multiple tasks
59	# (task_000015, task_000329, task_001321, task_000024, task_001781) fall into a

61	# identical result (often "(exit 0, no output captured)") — and burn the whole
62	# 80-step budget (exit_reason=budget_exceeded). The loop also poisons context:
63	# CompactionProcessor then summarises the loop and re-injects it, re-seeding the
64	# very repetition it described. This processor watches consecutive identical
