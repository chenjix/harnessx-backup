20	### Why
21	
22	Assigned focus was task_000109_09ddd96b (data_science): the agent wrote a Go
23	WAV-ETL pipeline that ran cleanly but produced a numerically wrong answer
24	(window 4 mean=128 instead of 0.0; anomaly.txt="1" instead of "4") and exited
25	via `no_tool_calls` fully convinced it was done. Strikingly, its own step-49
26	narration said "window 4 has all zeros" while the emitted JSON showed a mean of
27	128 — an internal contradiction it never reconciled. The stock
28	`CustomSelfVerifyProcessor` checklist fired at exit but only asked the agent to
29	"inspect the contents"; the agent re-listed files and exited without
30	re-deriving anything. This confident-exit-on-wrong-computed-value shape is the
31	dominant failure cluster on this benchmark: 30 of 40 failures exit via
32	`no_tool_calls`, and the data_science / scientific_computing / security ones
33	fail on *value* assertions, not missing files.
34	
35	The root parsing bug in task_000109 is a model capability gap that no harness
36	should fix by injecting WAV knowledge. But the *harness deficiency* is real: the
37	exit-time verification hook is too passive to make the agent notice its own
38	wrong output. The fix is a general verification discipline.
39	
40	### Changes
41	
42	- `processors/self_verify_crosscheck.py` — `SelfVerifyCrossCheckProcessor`,
43	  subclass of `CustomSelfVerifyProcessor` that reuses the parent's fires-once /
44	  exit-turn / keepalive mechanics and only swaps the injected checklist for one
45	  that adds (a) independent re-derivation of at least one output value by a
46	  different method and (b) a contradiction sweep between the agent's own
47	  reasoning and its emitted output. No task-specific constants.
48	- `config.yaml` — replaced `CustomSelfVerifyProcessor` with the subclass via
49	  `file://` absolute path.
