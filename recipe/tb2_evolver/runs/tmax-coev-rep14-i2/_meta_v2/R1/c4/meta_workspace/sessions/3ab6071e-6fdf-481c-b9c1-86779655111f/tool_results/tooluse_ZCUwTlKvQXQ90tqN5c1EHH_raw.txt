7	## Verifying computed results before you finish
8	
9	Whenever a task asks you to produce a computed or numeric result (a
10	fitted parameter, a statistic, a search/optimization outcome, an
11	aggregate, a transformed dataset), a solution that runs without errors
12	and looks plausible is NOT evidence that the value is correct. "Looks
13	physically sensible" or "matches the required format" only checks the
14	shape, never the number. Grading compares your value against an exact
15	reference within a tolerance, so a self-consistent but subtly-wrong
16	computation still scores zero. Before you declare such a task done:
17	
18	1. **Cross-validate with an independent method.** Recompute the key
19	   result a second, different way — a different library, a simpler
20	   closed-form check, a small hand-worked case, or a reference
21	   implementation in another language/tool available in the sandbox.
22	   If the two computations disagree, your primary implementation has a
23	   bug; find it before exiting. If they agree, you have real evidence.
24	
25	2. **Surface and test the ambiguous choices.** Deterministic pipelines
26	   are sensitive to details the prompt may under-specify: array
27	   indexing (0- vs 1-based, inclusive vs exclusive bounds), the exact
28	   order in which random draws are consumed under a fixed seed, which
29	   axis/variable is the regressor, rounding vs truncation, off-by-one
30	   in a search or loop range, and float precision. List the choices
31	   your code made at each such point, re-read the task to see which the
32	   specification actually pins down, and where it is genuinely
33	   ambiguous, try the alternative interpretation and compare — do not
34	   assume your first reading is the intended one.
35	
36	3. **Sanity-check against the data.** Confirm you read every input
37	   record you were supposed to (row/element counts), that no rows were
38	   silently dropped or misparsed, and that intermediate quantities are
39	   in a believable range given the inputs.
40	
41	Apply this discipline to computational tasks only; for tasks with no
42	checkable computed output, verify the required files exist at their
43	exact paths and hold the intended content, then finish.
