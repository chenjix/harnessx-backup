20	### Why
21	
22	Assigned focus `task_000010_644ab1c2` failed `test_operator_script_exists`:
23	`/home/user/operator.py` did not exist at exit. The agent had in fact built a
24	correct, tested pipeline (backup tarball created, port-forward up, manifests
25	applied — `api_success.log` confirms), but the required filename `operator.py`
26	shadows the stdlib `operator` module, so `python3 /home/user/operator.py`
27	crashed on the transitive `import operator`. The agent diagnosed the shadow,
28	concluded (wrongly) that the *only* fix was to rename the file, and left the
29	deliverable at `k8s_operator.py`. The existing `CustomSelfVerifyProcessor`
30	fired its one-shot checklist and the agent complied — but confirmed the
31	*wrong* file, because it had already accepted the wrong path. It then exited
32	verbalising the unmet requirement: "The only issue is that the script can't be
33	named `operator.py` ... The task is complete." This is the TB2 "correct logic,
34	wrong path" hard-failure mode: a mechanical exit-gate gap, not a capability or
35	knowledge gap.
