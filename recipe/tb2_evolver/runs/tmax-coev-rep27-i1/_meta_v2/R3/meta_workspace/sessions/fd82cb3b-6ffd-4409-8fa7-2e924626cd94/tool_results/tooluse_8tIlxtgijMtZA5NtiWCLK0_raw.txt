88	### Why
89	
90	R1 scored 43/50 and both R1-predicted service tasks (000028, 000958) flipped to
91	pass. Of the 7 remaining failures, exactly one is a recurring, structural,
92	harness-fixable pattern: **lingering background processes at container final
93	state**. The TB2 verifier runs pytest against the final container state after
94	the agent exits; some verifiers assert "no lingering `<service>` processes" via
95	`pgrep -f <name>` (which matches `<defunct>` zombies too). Agents that build a
96	daemon develop by launching it repeatedly in their persistent Bash session
97	(`./svc &`), leaving orphaned/zombie children that fail the clean-state
98	assertion even when the deliverable scripts are correct. The other 6 failures
99	are one-off model capability/logic gaps (log-rotation size, SQLite index
100	semantics, numeric deviation, grid optimization, tarball packaging) plus the
101	task_000010 `operator.py` stdlib-shadow task-design collision — none
102	harness-fixable without embedding domain knowledge, so logged as skips.

104	### Changes
105	
106	- `processors/service_verify.py` — extended R1's `ServiceVerifyDepsProcessor`
107	  with an additive checklist item #7: audit for and clear stray/zombie
108	  background processes the agent spawned during development. It explains that a
109	  `<defunct>` process cannot be signalled and is reaped only when its parent
