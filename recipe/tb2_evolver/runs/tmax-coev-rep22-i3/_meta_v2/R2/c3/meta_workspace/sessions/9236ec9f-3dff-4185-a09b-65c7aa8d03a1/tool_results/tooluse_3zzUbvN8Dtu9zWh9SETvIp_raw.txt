20	### Why
21	
22	Assigned focus `task_000010_644ab1c2` (system_administration, reward=0,
23	exit_reason=done, finished=no_tool_calls, 539.8s). The reward-0 root cause is
24	a task-design trap: the task requires the deliverable at `/home/user/operator.py`,
25	which shadows Python's stdlib `operator`; when the verifier runs pytest from
26	`/home/user` the shadow triggers a circular import and pytest cannot collect.
27	The agent's script is otherwise functionally correct. A harness fix for that
28	would need task-specific knowledge (rename/guard the file) and fails the
29	generalization test — logged as a task/capability trap, not patched.
30	
31	The generalizable harness gap the trajectory exposes is separate: while
32	agonizing over the operator.py conflict the model froze into **13 consecutive**
33	`stop_reason=length` no-tool-call turns (steps ~39-69), each a full 4096-token
34	re-narration — roughly 1/3 of the run's budget with zero forward action. The
35	existing `LengthTruncationRecoveryProcessor` fires (content collapsed to ~1964
36	chars, corrective nudge injected) but only ever *nudges* — it has no upper
37	bound and the model ignored the nudge all 13 turns. `CyclicLoopBreaker` keys on
38	tool-call cycles so a no-tool-call loop never enters its window; nothing in the
39	pipeline could terminate the loop.
40	
41	### Changes
42	
43	- `processors/length_recovery_escalating.py` — drop-in superset of the stock
44	  `LengthTruncationRecoveryProcessor`: keeps collapse + escalating-nudge
45	  verbatim, adds a `hard_stop_threshold` (default 6). After that many
46	  *consecutive* length-truncation no-tool-call turns, raises `LoopDetectedError`
47	  from `on_after_model` → clean `exit_reason='loop_detected'`, best output
48	  recovered, workspace preserved for the verifier. Counter resets on any tool
49	  call or non-length finish; `hard_stop_threshold<=0` restores stock behaviour.
50	- `config.yaml` — swapped the stock recipe `_target_` for the new `file://`
51	  processor and added `hard_stop_threshold: 6` (well above `repeat_threshold=2`
52	  so the nudge gets 4+ chances first). All other pipeline entries unchanged.
