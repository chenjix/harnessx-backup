18	### Why
19	
20	R0 scored 30/50. Five tasks all hit `exit_reason=budget_exceeded` at the
21	full 80-step cap (task_000010, task_000118, task_000264, task_001031,
22	task_001321). The shared root cause is a *reasoning-only truncation spiral*:
23	the model generates verbose narration that exceeds the per-turn `max_tokens`
24	(4096) cap with NO tool call, the run loop appends a passive "cut off by the

33	additionally exposed the "correct-logic wrong-path" hard-fail: it wrote
34	`/home/user/k8s_operator.py` while the verifier required
35	`/home/user/operator.py`.

40	  (singleton group `tmax_length_recovery`, so it REPLACES the R0 processor):

42	  truncations, mechanically injects a real generic Bash workspace-snapshot
