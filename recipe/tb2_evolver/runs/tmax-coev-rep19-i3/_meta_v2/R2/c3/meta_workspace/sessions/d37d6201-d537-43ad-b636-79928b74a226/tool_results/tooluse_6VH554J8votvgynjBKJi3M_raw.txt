168	### Why
169	
170	Assigned focus `task_000017_fed73abc` (scientific_computing) exited `done` in
171	17/80 steps with reward 0. Every mechanical step was correct: it found the
172	`temp *= 1.05` bug, changed it to `0.95`, confirmed `libnetcdf-dev`, compiled,
173	ran the annealer, and `ncdump`ed the trajectory. The single error: the objective
174	`score_position(pos) = (pos-42)^2 - 50` has its minimum at position 42, but the
175	agent took the LAST element of the `position` array (43) — a stochastic accepted
176	step, one index off the optimum — and never inspected the `score` variable at
177	all. Expected primer `AGCTAGCGCGCTAGC` (pos 42); committed `GCTAGCGCGCTAGCT`
178	(pos 43). This is a generalizable failure shape: for "return the optimum of a
179	search" deliverables the answer is the candidate at argmin/argmax of the recorded
180	objective, not the last iterate the search happened to log.
181	
182	### Changes
183	
184	- `processors/numeric_crosscheck_nudge.py` — copy of the R1/c3 NumericCrossCheckNudge
185	  with one added bullet (#2): if the deliverable is the optimum of a search/
186	  optimization (annealing, MCMC, gradient descent, genetic/random search, grid
187	  sweep), select it by inspecting the recorded score/energy/error series and
188	  taking the argmin/argmax candidate, and reconcile against the last-iterate
189	  value rather than assuming the last logged value is the optimum. Same
190	  exit-intent trigger, singleton, order 91, no tool calls.
191	- `config.yaml` — repoint NumericCrossCheckNudge `_target_` to the new file;
192	  updated the accompanying comment to describe the optimum-selection clause.
