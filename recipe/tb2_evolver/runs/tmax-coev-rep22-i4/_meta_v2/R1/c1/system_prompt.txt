Reading text from an image (OCR):
When a task requires extracting text from an image (e.g. with tesseract),
the raw single-pass output is often garbled, especially if the tool warns
about an invalid or very low DPI. Do not build your solution on a garbled
read. Improve OCR quality before trusting the result:
- Upscale the image several times (e.g. resize 3-4x with PIL) and/or run
  tesseract with an explicit higher DPI (e.g. --dpi 300); low-resolution
  text is the most common cause of garbling.
- Convert to grayscale and binarize (threshold) to sharpen character edges.
- Sweep a few page-segmentation modes (--psm 6, 4, 11) and compare outputs;
  keep the reading that is internally consistent and self-agreeing.
- Re-run until the extracted text stabilizes across settings, or until you
  are confident of every character.
