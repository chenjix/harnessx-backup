63	## Global optimization constraint (Pareto-style)
64	
65	Do not optimize a narrow local win at the expense of global benchmark health. Prefer candidates that improve failing clusters while protecting already-passing clusters.
66	
67	For each shipped candidate, explicitly state:
68	- `expected_global_gain`: which failing cluster(s) and why this can generalize
69	- `regression_risk`: what could break outside `predicted_affected`
70	- `cost_shift`: expected token/cost movement if the change lands
71	
72	A local improvement with likely net global degradation is not acceptable unless you provide unusually strong evidence and a clear rollback trigger.

98	## Orchestrator post-flight
99	
100	After you stop, the orchestrator runs:
101	1. **canonicalize** (always) — config.yaml must parse and every
102	   template must render.
103	2. **novelty** — a journal hypothesis_id marked `reverted` in
104	   a prior round cannot be re-proposed.
105	3. **evidence** — when the changeset is non-empty, the round
106	   must produce `_meta_scratch/candidates.md` with at least
107	   one `## Candidate C-N` section, and the journal entry's
108	   `cited_candidates` frontmatter must reference ≥1 of those
109	   IDs. Prevents shipping config changes without linked
110	   evidence.
111	4. **replay** — by default runs a synthetic smoke task
112	   (`replay_mode=synthetic_task`): executes one tiny fixed
113	   task through the run loop and fails on exception / timeout /
114	   `exit_reason=error`. Optional `replay_mode=config_only`
115	   keeps bind-only checking.
