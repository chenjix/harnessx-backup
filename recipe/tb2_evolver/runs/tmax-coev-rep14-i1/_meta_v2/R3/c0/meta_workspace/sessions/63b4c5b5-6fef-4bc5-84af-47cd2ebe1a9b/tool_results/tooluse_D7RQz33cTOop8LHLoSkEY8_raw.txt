23	(80 steps, reward=0). The verifier's `test_operator_script_exists` reports the
24	required deliverable `/home/user/operator.py` does not exist. Reading the

31	budget, and hits the wall without finalizing/verifying the deliverable at its

36	decisive moment: `CustomSelfVerifyProcessor` only fires on voluntary exit (never

46	  0.60 (soft) and 0.80 (hard), appends one user-role reminder to stop
47	  sub-component exploration and finalize + verify every required output at its
48	  EXACT specified path (no renaming/relocating deliverables). Fires each
49	  threshold once; only active when `max_steps >= 20`. Contract-clean (append
50	  one user message, mirrors `TaskTimeReminderProcessor`; no removals, no
51	  system-prompt mutation).

53	  (`warn_at: [0.6, 0.8]`, `min_budget: 20`, `hard_threshold: 0.8`); rest of the
