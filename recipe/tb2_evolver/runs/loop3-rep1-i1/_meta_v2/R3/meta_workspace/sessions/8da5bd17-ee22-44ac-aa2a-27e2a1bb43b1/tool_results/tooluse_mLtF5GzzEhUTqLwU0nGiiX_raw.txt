136	### Discovery (latent bug, not this round's failure cause)
137	
138	`TaskTimeReminderProcessor` and `EnvironmentContextInjector` are wired
139	with `timeout_seconds` **only** on the default-builder path. When an
140	evolved `config.yaml` is loaded (`agent.py:189-192` uses
141	`HarnessConfig.from_yaml_file` without re-injecting `task_timeout`),
142	both deserialize with `timeout_seconds=None`, so `on_step_start`
143	short-circuits (`harness.py:246`) and the time-budget mechanism is
144	dead in every evolved run. Fix requires editing `agent.py` — outside
145	meta-agent write scope; the per-task timeout is not reachable from any
146	processor event. Logged in `NEEDS_FROM_HUMAN.md` #1. Not urgent for
147	score (would not have flipped any R2 failure).
