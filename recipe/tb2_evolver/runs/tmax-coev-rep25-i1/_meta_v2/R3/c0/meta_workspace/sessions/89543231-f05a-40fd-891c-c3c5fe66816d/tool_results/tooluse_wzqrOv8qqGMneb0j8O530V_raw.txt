298	### Why
299	
300	Assigned focus: complementary synthesis of the live pivots. Reading every
301	pivot: n1 (R2/c0) and n2 (R2) config.yaml are BYTE-IDENTICAL and share one
302	trajectory dir, so there are only two distinct harnesses, R0 and
303	"R0 plus StuckReasoningRecoveryProcessor". current_config points at R0. The
304	per-task diff is R0=25/50 vs r1-fe-c0=29/50 (all status=ok): +6 gains, -2
305	losses (net +4). The decisive attribution finding: the StuckReasoning nudge
306	("LOOP DETECTED" / "STILL LOOPING") fired ZERO times across all 50 tasks in the
307	r1-fe-c0 run, including on every gained and every lost task. So the +6/-2 swing
308	is entirely run-to-run variance, not a mechanism effect; the guard's
309	repeat_threshold=12 is never reached because LengthTruncationRecovery
310	(threshold=2) breaks the common loops first. The synthesis focus (graft a
311	complementary mechanism to repair the winning lineage's unique losses) is
312	therefore unsupported: the two losses (1089, 1536) are stochastic correctness
313	differences the inert guard did not cause and no harness lever attributably
314	repairs. Per the generalization contract, that earns adopting the best live
315	lineage as-is rather than inventing a special case.
