20	### Why
21	
22	Assigned focus task_000069_41f1682c fails despite exiting cleanly
23	(`no_tool_calls`/`done`, reward 0). The task said to "redirect
24	(**append**)" the executable's stdout to `deployment.log`; the agent used
25	`>` (truncate). Its own idempotency test ran the script twice, saw one log
26	line, and *declared success* — but the verifier ran the script twice and
27	expected the log to grow to exactly 2 lines while `routes.txt` stayed at 2
28	lines (config idempotent, log append-only). The agent conflated "idempotent
29	persistent state" with "truncate the log". The stock `CustomSelfVerifyProcessor`
30	checklist asked "does your solution address every requirement", but let the
31	agent *assert* compliance in prose without ever testing the specific literal
32	behaviour. This is the dominant shape in the round's 42 failures: the large
33	`no_tool_calls`/`done`+`reward=0` cluster is mostly premature commits where the
34	verifier fails on an exact format/count/behavioural-verb mismatch the agent
35	never actually exercised.

37	### Changes
38	
39	- `processors/strict_self_verify.py` — new `StrictSelfVerifyProcessor`
40	  (`MultiHookProcessor`); same one-shot keepalive mechanics + singleton group
41	  (`tb2_self_verify`) as the stock processor, but injects a checklist that
42	  forces (1) verbatim enumeration of the task's LITERAL requirements (exact
43	  formats, counts, behavioural verbs like "append"/"idempotent", thresholds)
44	  and (2) a proof command per requirement whose output is compared to the spec,
45	  explicitly rejecting a single happy-path run.
46	- `config.yaml` — replaced the
47	  `benchmarks.terminal_bench_2.harness.CustomSelfVerifyProcessor` entry with a
48	  `file://` reference to the new class. No other pipeline change.
