20	### Why
21	
22	Assigned focus `task_000010_644ab1c2` failed with `exit_reason=budget_exceeded`
23	(80 steps, reward=0). The verifier's `test_operator_script_exists` reports the
24	required deliverable `/home/user/operator.py` does not exist. Reading the
25	trajectory: the agent created `operator.py` (msg 19), then **renamed it away**
26	with `mv /home/user/operator.py /home/user/k8s_operator.py` (msg 23), and spent
27	the entire back half of the run (msgs 40–70) in a port-forward / zombie-process
28	debugging spiral, never restoring the required path. This is the shared shape of
29	the round's `budget_exceeded` cluster (also task_000118, task_000206,
30	task_000958): the agent gets absorbed in one sub-component, burns the step
31	budget, and hits the wall without finalizing/verifying the deliverable at its
32	exact specified path.
33	
34	The harness has the *knowledge* (system prompt + `CustomSelfVerifyProcessor`
35	both say "verify exact output paths") but no *delivery mechanism* at the
36	decisive moment: `CustomSelfVerifyProcessor` only fires on voluntary exit (never
37	reached in a budget_exceeded run), and `TaskTimeReminderProcessor` is inert in
38	the tmax path (`timeout_seconds` is never injected by `harness_runner._run_async`,
39	so `self._timeout` stays None). The step budget (`BaseTask.max_steps=80`) has no
40	reminder at all.
41	
42	### Changes
43	
44	- `processors/step_budget_reminder.py` — new `StepBudgetReminderProcessor`
45	  (`MultiHookProcessor`): on `on_step_start`, when `step_id/max_steps` crosses
46	  0.60 (soft) and 0.80 (hard), appends one user-role reminder to stop
47	  sub-component exploration and finalize + verify every required output at its
48	  EXACT specified path (no renaming/relocating deliverables). Fires each
49	  threshold once; only active when `max_steps >= 20`. Contract-clean (append
50	  one user message, mirrors `TaskTimeReminderProcessor`; no removals, no
51	  system-prompt mutation).
52	- `config.yaml` — register the processor after `TaskTimeReminderProcessor`
53	  (`warn_at: [0.6, 0.8]`, `min_budget: 20`, `hard_threshold: 0.8`); rest of the
54	  pipeline byte-identical to R0. `system_prompt.txt` sibling copied unchanged.
