6	# Journal — cross-round memory
7	
8	A markdown file (the caller passes its path as `memo_path`). Each
9	evolve appends one `## Round N — <label>` section. The file is the
10	meta-agent's only memory across rounds — there is no persistent
11	conversation, and the orchestrator fills in gating outcomes between
12	rounds so the next agent can see which past bets paid off.
13	
14	The orchestrator pre-renders a machine index at
15	`_meta_scratch/CONTEXT.md` (when the journal has prior entries):
16	lever scoreboard, recent hypotheses, reverted bets. Read that
17	first — it's a cheap way to see what's been tried without scanning
18	the full journal prose.
19	
20	## On entry — read once
21	
22	1. `Read <memo_path>` — full cross-round history, prose included.
23	2. `Read _meta_scratch/CONTEXT.md` (when present) — compact index of
24	   lever usage, attribution, and reverted hypotheses.
25	
26	Look for:
27	- Hypotheses with `gating_outcome: reverted` → do not re-propose
28	  without new evidence (hard invariant #4 variant).
29	- The lever scoreboard's prediction hit rate → if Action landed
30	  1/12 times while Instruction landed 4/6, update your prior.
31	- Outstanding `needs_from_human` items (free-prose; scan for them).
32	
33	## On exit — append one section
34	
35	Required shape:
36	
37	```markdown
38	## Round N — <short label in 2-5 words>
39	
40	<!-- journal:frontmatter
41	round: N
42	timestamp: 2026-04-27T15:30:00Z    # ISO-8601 UTC; orchestrator can override
43	hypothesis_id: h_<short slug>      # stable across related rounds
44	levers: [action]                   # subset of {configuration, control, action, instruction}
45	predicted_affected: [task_a, task_b]
46	gating_outcome: pending             # orchestrator back-fills next round
47	gating_attribution: pending         # orchestrator back-fills next round
48	expected_global_gain: "<one-line cluster-level upside>"
49	regression_risk: "<main risk outside predicted_affected>"
50	cost_shift: "<expected token/cost delta or direction>"
51	rollback_trigger: "<observable signal to revert next round>"
52	-->
53	
54	### Why
55	
56	One paragraph on the pattern that motivated the round — the
57	cluster of trajectories and the shape they share.
