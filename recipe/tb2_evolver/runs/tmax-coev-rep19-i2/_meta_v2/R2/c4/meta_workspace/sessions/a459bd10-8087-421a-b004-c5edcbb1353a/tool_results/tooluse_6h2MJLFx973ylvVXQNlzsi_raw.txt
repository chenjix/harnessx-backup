18	### Why
19	
20	Assigned focus `task_000111_cbada64a` (scientific_computing) fails: the agent
21	wrote structurally-correct OLS+bootstrap C++, ran it in 7 steps / 16.9s, and
22	committed `m=2.5056` where the verifier expected `m≈2.5997`. OLS is
23	deterministic in the data, so the miss is an *interpretation* error (parsing /
24	index / formula), not a harness crash. The same shape recurs across the
25	numeric cluster: `task_001330` (Monte-Carlo fit, m=0.048 vs 0.050, 7 steps)
26	and `task_001937` (grid opt, 60 vs 50, 15 steps). In every case the agent
27	exited fast (`no_tool_calls`) and its one-shot `_tb2_self_verify` turn only
28	re-`cat`'d the output and re-confirmed format — it never re-audited the
29	algorithmic interpretation where the answer actually went wrong. The built-in
30	`CustomSelfVerifyProcessor` prompt (read-only) is oriented to file
31	existence/format, which is the wrong lens for numeric-computation tasks.
32	
33	### Changes
34	
35	- `processors/numeric_result_audit.py` — new `NumericResultAuditProcessor`
36	  (`MultiHookProcessor`). On `on_after_tool`, when the built-in synthetic
37	  `_tb2_self_verify` tool fires, it appends a computational-interpretation
38	  audit directive (re-read the method; check draw-order/seed, index/axis
39	  conventions, parsing edge cases, formula/rounding variant; recompute an
40	  independent second way; try the alternative reading of any ambiguity). Fires
41	  at most once, and only when the session produced a value-agnostic
42	  numeric-result signature. Contract-safe: augments `event.result` only.
43	- `config.yaml` — register the new processor immediately after
44	  `CustomSelfVerifyProcessor` (`_order=95`, after self-verify's `_order=90`).

63	### Uncertainty
64	
65	Honest weak point: for `task_000111`/`task_001330` the misread is subtle, so
66	re-reading may not deterministically flip them — the directive raises the
67	probability of catching an interpretation error, it does not guarantee it (the
68	retroactive check is partial-yes, strongest on `task_001937`-style
69	"alternative reading" misses). The intervention is deliberately free on tasks
70	where it doesn't fire, so the downside is bounded. If the numeric cluster
71	doesn't move next round while step-counts rise, revert per the trigger. This
72	was NOT patched by embedding any task answer or constant — it is a
73	generalizable strategy nudge gated on a value-agnostic signal.
