20	### Why
21	
22	R0 scored 29/50 (58%). Of 21 failures, 6 hit `exit_reason=budget_exceeded`
23	at the full 80-step budget, spanning system_administration, data_querying,
24	scientific_computing, and data_processing. Every one failed the verifier on
25	a "required output file missing" assertion — the agent explored/thrashed the
26	whole budget and left **nothing on disk**. task_000264's own words: "I've
27	been stuck in a loop trying the same query"; then three "Your previous
28	response was cut off... continue" turns with no file write, ending at step 80
29	with `top_managers.csv` / `query_plan.txt` missing. The pipeline ships a
30	`TaskTimeReminderProcessor` but the recipe runner never sets its
31	`timeout_seconds`, so its `on_step_start` returns early every step — the
32	deadline nudge is inert. The budget actually enforced is *steps* (max_steps=80),
33	which nothing tracks.
34	
35	### Changes
36	
37	- `processors/step_budget_deadline.py` — new `StepBudgetDeadlineProcessor`
38	  (`MultiHookProcessor`). On `on_step_start`, computes `step_id / task.max_steps`
39	  and injects escalating user-role reminders at 65% (early) and 85%
40	  (critical) telling the agent to commit its best-effort deliverable to the
41	  required paths NOW and `ls`-verify. Task-agnostic — names no paths/domains.
42	- `config.yaml` — register the processor at `_order=7` (right after the inert
43	  time reminder) via absolute `file://` path, `warn_at: [0.65, 0.85]`.
44	
45	### Evidence
46	
47	- task_000264_ab8c7253: `exit_reason=budget_exceeded`, steps=80, 953s;
48	  verifier `top_managers.csv missing` + `query_plan.txt missing` + no index.
49	  Body last msgs: "I keep getting the same result" / "stuck in a loop".
50	- task_001031_a8f0eb37: `budget_exceeded`, 80 steps, 2336s; verifier
51	  `Results file missing at /home/user/results.json`.
52	- task_000010_644ab1c2: `budget_exceeded`, 80 steps; verifier `operator.py
53	  does not exist` + `api_success.log does not exist`.
54	- task_000118_3043e92d / task_000958_4bb2b05d / task_001321_658ce4a8: all
55	  `budget_exceeded` at 80 steps (same shape).
56	- Root-cause harness fact: harness.py:246 — `TaskTimeReminderProcessor`
57	  early-returns when `timeout_seconds` is unset, which the runner never sets.
58	
59	### Uncertainty
60	
61	The reminder helps only if a runnable-but-imperfect deliverable exists in the
62	model's head by ~step 52; on tasks where the agent is genuinely lost (no valid
63	approach at all) it won't produce a passing file. Watch attribution: if
64	predicted tasks stay `still_F` while a slow passing task regresses, the nudge
65	is disrupting more than it helps — revert per trigger above.
