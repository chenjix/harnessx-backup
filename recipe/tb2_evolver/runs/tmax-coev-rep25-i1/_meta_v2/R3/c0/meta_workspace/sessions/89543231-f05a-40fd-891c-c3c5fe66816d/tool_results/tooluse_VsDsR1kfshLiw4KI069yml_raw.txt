58	## Global optimization constraint (Pareto-style)
59	
60	Do not optimize a narrow local win at the expense of global benchmark health. Prefer candidates that improve failing clusters while protecting already-passing clusters.
61	
62	For each shipped candidate, explicitly state:
63	- `expected_global_gain`: which failing cluster(s) and why this can generalize
64	- `regression_risk`: what could break outside `predicted_affected`
65	- `cost_shift`: expected token/cost movement if the change lands
66	
67	A local improvement with likely net global degradation is not acceptable unless you provide unusually strong evidence and a clear rollback trigger.

93	## Orchestrator post-flight
94	
95	After you stop, the orchestrator runs:
96	1. **canonicalize** (always) — config.yaml must parse and every
97	   template must render.
98	2. **novelty** — a journal hypothesis_id marked `reverted` in
99	   a prior round cannot be re-proposed.
100	3. **evidence** — when the changeset is non-empty, the round
101	   must produce `_meta_scratch/candidates.md` with at least
102	   one `## Candidate C-N` section, and the journal entry's
103	   `cited_candidates` frontmatter must reference ≥1 of those
104	   IDs. Prevents shipping config changes without linked
105	   evidence.
106	4. **replay** — by default runs a synthetic smoke task
107	   (`replay_mode=synthetic_task`): executes one tiny fixed
108	   task through the run loop and fails on exception / timeout /
109	   `exit_reason=error`. Optional `replay_mode=config_only`
110	   keeps bind-only checking.
