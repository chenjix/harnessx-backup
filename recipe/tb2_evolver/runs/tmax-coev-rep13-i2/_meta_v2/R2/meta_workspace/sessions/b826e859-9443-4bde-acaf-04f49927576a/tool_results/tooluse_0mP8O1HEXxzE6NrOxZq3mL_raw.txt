30	## Global optimization constraint (Pareto-style)
31	
32	Do not optimize a narrow local win at the expense of global benchmark health. Prefer candidates that improve failing clusters while protecting already-passing clusters.
33	
34	For each shipped candidate, explicitly state:
35	- `expected_global_gain`: which failing cluster(s) and why this can generalize
36	- `regression_risk`: what could break outside `predicted_affected`
37	- `cost_shift`: expected token/cost movement if the change lands
38	
39	A local improvement with likely net global degradation is not acceptable unless you provide unusually strong evidence and a clear rollback trigger.

65	## Orchestrator post-flight
66	
67	After you stop, the orchestrator runs:
68	1. **canonicalize** (always) — config.yaml must parse and every
69	   template must render.
70	2. **novelty** — a journal hypothesis_id marked `reverted` in

72	3. **evidence** — when the changeset is non-empty, the round
73	   must produce `_meta_scratch/candidates.md` with at least
74	   one `## Candidate C-N` section, and the journal entry's
75	   `cited_candidates` frontmatter must reference ≥1 of those
76	   IDs. Prevents shipping config changes without linked
77	   evidence.
78	4. **replay** — by default runs a synthetic smoke task
79	   (`replay_mode=synthetic_task`): executes one tiny fixed
80	   task through the run loop and fails on exception / timeout /
81	   `exit_reason=error`. Optional `replay_mode=config_only`
82	   keeps bind-only checking.
