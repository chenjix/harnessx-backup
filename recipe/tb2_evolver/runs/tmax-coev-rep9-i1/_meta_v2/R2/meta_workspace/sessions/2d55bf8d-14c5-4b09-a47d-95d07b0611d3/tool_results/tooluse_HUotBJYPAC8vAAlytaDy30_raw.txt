20	### Why
21	
22	R0 scored 29/50 (0.58). Beyond the diverse capability failures (sysadmin 0/5,
23	etc.), one structural harness deficiency dominated the wall-clock and stability
24	picture: **byte-identical tool-call stalls with no hard stop**. task_001031
25	ran for 5868s (98 min) and ended in `exit_reason=error` after emitting the
26	exact same 2133-char reasoning + the byte-identical `cat > analyze.py` Bash
27	command 38 times consecutively, each timing out at 120s. Two more tasks
28	(task_000863, task_001321) hit the 80-step budget cap the same way (33x and
29	20x identical consecutive Bash calls). The pipeline's `CustomEditToolProcessor`
30	only emits a soft warning, which the model demonstrably ignored — it even
31	narrated "I've been stuck in a loop" and kept issuing the identical command.
32	
33	### Changes
34	
35	- `config.yaml` — added `harnessx.processors.control.loop_detection.LoopDetectionProcessor`
36	  with `warn_threshold=4`, `threshold=6`, `name_warn_threshold=10`,
37	  `compaction_drop_threshold=5`. Strategy-1 (exact name+inputs fingerprint)
38	  raises `LoopDetectedError` on the 6th consecutive byte-identical tool call →
39	  clean `exit_reason=loop_detected` (runloop.py:781). No new code authored;
40	  wiring an existing, tested processor.
