55	## Global optimization constraint (Pareto-style)
56	
57	Do not optimize a narrow local win at the expense of global benchmark health. Prefer candidates that improve failing clusters while protecting already-passing clusters.
58	
59	For each shipped candidate, explicitly state:
60	- `expected_global_gain`: which failing cluster(s) and why this can generalize
61	- `regression_risk`: what could break outside `predicted_affected`
62	- `cost_shift`: expected token/cost movement if the change lands
63	
64	A local improvement with likely net global degradation is not acceptable unless you provide unusually strong evidence and a clear rollback trigger.
